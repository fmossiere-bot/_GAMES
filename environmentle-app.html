How it works

Three minutes.
Three steps.

1
Play today's challenge
Each day has a short themed challenge — a quiz, a sorting game, or a carbon calculator. Takes under 3 minutes.
2
Learn something surprising
After each question or round, we show you the fact behind it — something you can actually use. No lectures, just good information.
3
Commit to one action
Pick one thing you can do this week — tailored to what came up in your game. Small, realistic, and worth extra points.

Why it matters

The gap between
knowing and doing

Most people in Ireland care about the environment — but feel stuck. Environmentle is built around the research that explains why, and what actually helps.
