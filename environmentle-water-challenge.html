    One thing to keep in perspective

    Farming uses somewhere between 70 and 87 per cent of the fresh water people consume, depending on whether you count what is withdrawn or what is used up. Everything you do at home sits inside the small remainder. That's why swapping one beef meal does more than a shorter shower.

    That doesn't make your choices pointless. It does mean water is mostly a systems problem: what gets grown, where it gets grown, and who's allowed to pump. Shorter showers won't fix that on their own, and I'm not going to pretend otherwise.

    The food and goods figures are global averages. The home ones are Irish or European, as each card says. All of them have real uncertainty behind them, and a few are disputed between sources. I've noted which ones, and why, in the data behind the game.
